# Kyverna Therapeutics

> Kyverna Therapeutics (Nasdaq: KYTX) is a patient-centered, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Emeryville, California, developing engineered T cell therapies for autoimmune disease. Kyverna adapts CD19 CAR T-cell therapy — originally developed for B cell cancers — to deeply deplete the B cells that drive autoimmunity, with the goal of durable, drug-free remission.
